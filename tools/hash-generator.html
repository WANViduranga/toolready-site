A note on MD5 and SHA-1

MD5 and SHA-1 are included for compatibility and checksum verification (e.g. confirming a downloaded file matches a publisher's listed MD5), but both are cryptographically broken and should never be used for password storage or security-sensitive hashing. Use SHA-256 or higher for anything security-related.
