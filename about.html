About ToolReady

A free collection of calculators and tools, built to answer everyday questions quickly — no sign-up, no clutter.

What this is

ToolReady is a small set of calculators and tools split into three areas: taxes and finance for Sri Lanka, everyday calculators used anywhere in the world, and a few IT/security utilities. The goal is simple — pick a tool, get your answer immediately, and move on.

How the calculators work

Everything runs directly in your browser. When you type a number into a calculator, nothing is sent to a server — the math happens on your device, instantly, and nothing you enter is stored or logged. The one exception is the IP lookup tool, which by its nature needs to ask an external service what your public IP address is.

Keeping rates accurate

Calculators involving Sri Lanka tax brackets, EPF/ETF and similar statutory figures are checked against official sources and dated on each page. Rates do change — if you spot one that looks out of date, the contact page has a way to flag it.

Who built this

ToolReady is an independent project built and maintained by one person with a background in IT support and cybersecurity, aiming to make everyday calculations a little easier for both Sri Lankan and global visitors.
