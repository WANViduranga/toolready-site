Contact

Spotted an outdated rate, found a bug, or want to suggest a new calculator? Reach out below.

The quickest way to reach us is by email:

contact@gettoolready.com

We aim to respond within a few days. For an outdated rate, please mention which calculator and, if you have it, a link to the official source with the correct figure — that makes it much faster to verify and fix.

What's useful to include

Bug reports: which page, what you entered, and what went wrong. Rate corrections: which number, and where you found the current official figure. Tool suggestions: what calculation you were trying to do that isn't covered yet.
