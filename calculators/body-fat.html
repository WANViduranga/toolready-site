Measurement tips

Neck: just below the larynx (Adam's apple). Waist: at the navel for men, at the narrowest point for women. Hip (women only): at the widest point around the glutes. Keep the tape horizontal and don't suck in your stomach — measure naturally. This is an estimate, not a medical diagnosis; for precise body composition, methods like DEXA scans are more accurate.
