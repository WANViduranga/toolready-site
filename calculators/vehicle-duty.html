How this estimate is built

Customs Duty is applied to the CIF value first. Excise Duty is then added on top, tiered by engine size for petrol/diesel vehicles (electric vehicles use a separate, typically much lower rate). PAL and SSCL apply as standard levies, and VAT is applied last on the cumulative total. Real customs assessments may include additional components not shown here.
