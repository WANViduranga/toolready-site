BMI ranges

Below 18.5: underweight  ·  18.5–24.9: healthy range  ·  25–29.9: overweight  ·  30+: obese. BMI is a general screening tool — it doesn't account for muscle mass, age, or body composition, so it's not a diagnosis. Talk to a healthcare provider for personalized guidance.
