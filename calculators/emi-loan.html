How EMI is calculated

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ ÷ ((1+r)ⁿ − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly payments. This assumes a fixed rate for the full term — check with your lender if yours is variable.
