How this is calculated

A Deed of Transfer is taxed at 3% on the first Rs 100,000 of value and 4% on the remainder. A Deed of Gift gets a lower rate: 3% on the first Rs 50,000, then 2% on the remainder. A mortgage bond is taxed at Rs 1 per Rs 1,000 of the loan amount (0.1%). The duty is calculated on the higher of the declared price or the government-assessed value.
