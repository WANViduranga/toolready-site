EPF vs ETF, in plain terms

EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) is a retirement savings pot — 8% comes out of your salary, and your employer adds 12% on top, so 20% of your salary total goes in every month. ETF (Employees' Trust Fund) is a separate, employer-only 3% contribution — you don't pay into it, but it's still yours at retirement.
