Three ways to create your signature

  • Draw it with your mouse, finger or stylus, in black or blue ink.
  • Type your name and pick a handwriting-style or elegant italic look.
  • Upload a photo of your real signature — the white paper background is removed for you.

Once created, the signature is placed wherever you click, and you can drag it to move it or pull the corner handle to resize it. Your last signature is remembered while the page is open, so signing several pages is quick. You can add the date and a check mark in the same session.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Click Sign a PDF now and choose your file.
  2. Create your signature, press Use signature, then click where it should go.
  3. Press Download PDF to save the signed copy to your device.

Private by design

A signed agreement is exactly the sort of document you shouldn't upload to a stranger's server. Here, signing happens inside your browser: the document and your signature never leave your device, and the editor page loads no ads or trackers.

About legal validity. This adds an image of your signature to the PDF — a simple electronic signature. Many everyday documents accept that, but whether it is enough depends on the document, the other party and your country's rules. It is not a certified digital signature and it creates no audit trail. If you're unsure, check with whoever asked you to sign. This is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Is it free to download the signed PDF?

Yes — no watermark and no payment step.

Can I sign on my phone?

Yes. Draw with your finger in the signature box, then tap on the page to place it.

Is my signature stored anywhere?

No. It exists only in your browser while the page is open and is gone when you close or reload it.

Can I sign a form that has fields?

Yes. Fill in the blue fields, then add your signature on top — all in one session.
